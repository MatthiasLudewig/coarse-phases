# Wannier basis

```{warning}

```

This is a stub for the relation between localization of occupied states and
coarse topological obstructions.

For an orthogonal projection $P$ on a geometric module, a Wannier basis is an
orthonormal basis of $\operatorname{Ran}P$ whose vectors are localized near
specified centres. In periodic problems it is usually required to arise from
lattice translates of finitely many vectors. In a coarse setting the centres
may instead form a locally finite subset, with a stated uniform localization
condition.

A full definition must specify the type of localization: compact support,
exponential decay, or a weaker decay condition. These lead to different
existence questions and should not be identified without hypotheses.

The future article will discuss the periodic Bloch-bundle picture, generalized
Wannier bases, and obstructions detected by
{ref}`Roe-algebra K-theory <article-coarse-k-theory>`. See
{ref}`Ewert–Meyer <bib-ewert-meyer>` and the supplied Wannier material for the
coarse viewpoint.
