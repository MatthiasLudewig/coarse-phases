# Coarse cyclic homology

This is a stub for the relation between cyclic homology of controlled operator
algebras and ordinary coarse homology.

For the {prf:ref}`finite propagation locally trace-class algebra
<def-trace-roe-algebra>`, operator products and traces give character maps
relating cyclic chains to {ref}`coarse chains <article-ordinary-coarse-homology>`.
Dually, a coarse cohomology cocycle can give a cyclic cocycle. Pairing these
with algebraic $K$-theory classes produces the trace expressions associated
to {ref}`coarsely transverse partitions <article-coarse-transversality>`.

The full article will specify the algebra and completion, the cyclic chain
complex, the character map, and its compatibility with Mayer–Vietoris
boundaries. The expression *coarse cyclic homology* should not conceal these
choices. See {ref}`Roe <bib-roe-cohomology>`, §4, and
{ref}`Ludewig–Thiang <bib-large-scale-trace>`, §4.
