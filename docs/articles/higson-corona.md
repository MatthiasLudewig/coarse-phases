# Higson corona

The Higson corona is a compact Hausdorff space which may be viewed as the "boundary at infinity" of a coarse space $X$, akin to the Stone–Čech compactification of a topological space.

## Higson functions and the corona

Let $(X, \cB, \cC)$ be a bornological coarse space.

A bounded complex-valued function $f$ on $X$ *vanishes at infinity* if for each $\varepsilon > 0$, there exists a bounded set $B \subseteq X$ such that $|f(x)| \leq \varepsilon$ whenever $x \in X \setminus B$.
The $C^*$-algebra of functions vanishing at infinity is denoted by $\ell^\infty(\cB)$.
It may be written as the norm closure of the set of functions in $\ell^\infty(X)$ with bounded support.

For a bounded complex-valued function $f$ on $X$ and an entourage $E$, the $E$-variation of $f$ is
$$
\Var_E(f, Y)
 =\sup\{|f(x)-f(y)|:(x,y)\in E\cap(Y \times Y)\},
 \qquad
 Y \subseteq X.
$$

A bounded function $f:X\to\C$ has *vanishing variation at infinity* if, for every entourage $E$ and every $\varepsilon>0$, there is a bounded $B$ such that $\Var_E(f, X\setminus B)\leq\varepsilon$.
Such functions are also called *Higson functions*.

Functions with vanishing variation at infinity form a unital $C^*$-algebra $\ell^\infty_h(X)\subseteq \ell^\infty(X)$ containing $\ell^\infty(\cB)$ as an ideal.



```{prf:definition} Higson compactification and corona
:nonumber:
:label: def-higson-corona

The *Higson corona* $\partial X$ is the Gelfand spectrum of the commutative unital $C^*$-algebra $\ell^\infty_h(X) / \ell^\infty(\cB)$.
```

By the Gelfand--Naimark theorem, $\partial X$ is a compact Hausdorff space.

## Higson-dominated coronas

```{prf:definition} Higson-dominated compactification
:nonumber:
:label: def-higson-dominated-corona

A *Higson-dominated corona* is the Gelfand spectrum of the commutative $C^*$-algebra $A /\ell^\infty(\cB)$, where $A \subseteq \ell^\infty_h(X)$ is some unital $C^*$-subalgebra containing $\ell^\infty(\cB)$.
```

For $\R^n$, the bounded transforms $y_i := x_i/(1+|x|)$, $i=1, \dots, n$ of the coordinate functions have vanishing variation at infinity.
Letting $A$ be the commutative $C^*$-algebra generated by $y_i$ and $\ell^\infty(\cB)$, we obtain a Higson-dominated corona of $\R^n$ homeomorphic to $S^{n-1}$, called the *spherical corona* of $\R^n$.


## Coronas associated to big families

The construction extends to coarse spaces without a topology.
Let $X$ be a coarse space and $\cY$ a {prf:ref}`big family <def-big-family>`.
We define $\ell^\infty(\cY)$ as the norm closure in $\ell^\infty(X)$ of those functions supported on some member of $\cY$.

A complex-valued function $f \in \ell^\infty(X)$ has *bounded variation at infinity away from* $\cY$ if for each $\varepsilon >0$, there exists $Y \in \cY$ such that $\Var_E(f, X \setminus Y) \leq \varepsilon$.
The set $\ell^\infty_{\cY}(X)$ of functions with bounded variation away from $\cY$ is a unital $C^*$-subalgebra of $\ell^\infty(X)$ containing $\ell^\infty(\cY)$ as an ideal.

```{prf:definition} Coarse corona of a big family
:nonumber:
:label: def-big-family-corona

The *coarse $\cY$-corona* is the compact Hausdorff space defined as the Gelfand spectrum of the unital commutative $C^*$-algebra $\ell^\infty_{\cY}(X)/\ell^\infty(\cY)$.
```

This specializes to the Higson corona if $\cY=\cB$.
This notion was introduced in [Bunke–Ludewig, Coronas and Callias type operators in coarse geometry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.01646).
