# Freed–Hopkins classification

This is a stub for the stable-homotopy formulation of invertible topological
phases due to Freed and Hopkins.

The framework of
[Freed–Hopkins, Reflection positivity and invertible topological phases](https://arxiv.org/abs/1604.06527)
encodes spacetime dimension and symmetry type
using bordism spectra and describes deformation classes of reflection-positive
invertible theories through stable homotopy theory. Applying this to microscopic
lattice phases requires the connection with a suitable low-energy field theory;
it is not an unconditional classification of every lattice Hamiltonian.

The full article will specify the symmetry data, the relevant Thom spectrum,
the Anderson-dual formulation, and the comparison sought with coarse models of
phases.
