A **bornological coarse space** is a triple $(X,\mathcal E,\mathcal B)$, where
$\mathcal E$ is a coarse structure on $X$ and $\mathcal B$ is a bornology on $X$,
such that

$$
E[B]=\{x\in X:\text{ there is }b\in B\text{ with }(x,b)\in E\}
\quad\text{belongs to }\mathcal B
$$

for every $E\in\mathcal E$ and $B\in\mathcal B$. We call this condition
**compatibility**. In this wiki, we abbreviate bornological coarse space to
**coarse space**.
