# Reading list

These two starting references connect the geometric and physical sides of the
wiki. The bibliography will grow with the reviewed articles.

(bib-bunke-engel)=
## Bornological coarse geometry

Ulrich Bunke and Alexander Engel, *Homotopy theory with bornological coarse
spaces*, arXiv:1607.03657, version 4 (2020).
[Original manuscript](https://arxiv.org/abs/1607.03657v4).
The basic definitions are in §2.1; coarse equivalences are discussed in §3.2.

(bib-ewert-meyer)=
## Roe algebras and phases

Eske Ellen Ewert and Ralf Meyer, *Coarse geometry and topological phases*,
Communications in Mathematical Physics **366** (2019), 1069–1098.
[Article DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00220-019-03303-z),
[preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1802.05579).
