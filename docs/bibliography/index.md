# Bibliography

Public papers cited for results whose proofs are not given in the wiki.
Definitions, self-contained arguments, and standard textbook material do not require literature citations.
Each article links directly to the papers it uses.

(bib-bunke-engel)=
## Bunke–Engel: bornological coarse homotopy theory

Ulrich Bunke and Alexander Engel, [Homotopy theory with bornological coarse spaces](https://arxiv.org/abs/1607.03657), Lecture Notes in Mathematics **2269**, Springer, 2020.

(bib-callias)=
## Bunke–Ludewig: coronas and Callias operators

Ulrich Bunke and Matthias Ludewig, [Coronas and Callias type operators in coarse geometry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.01646).

(bib-large-scale-trace)=
## Ludewig–Thiang: large-scale trace quantization

Matthias Ludewig and Guo Chuan Thiang, [Large-scale quantization of trace I: Finite propagation operators](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.10957).

(bib-qca)=
## Ludewig: the coarse homology theory of QCA

Matthias Ludewig, [Quantum cellular automata are a coarse homology theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.10501).

(bib-gnvw)=
## Gross–Nesme–Vogts–Werner: the one-dimensional index

David Gross, Vincent Nesme, Holger Vogts, and Reinhard F. Werner, [Index theory of one dimensional quantum walks and cellular automata](https://arxiv.org/abs/0910.3675), Communications in Mathematical Physics **310** (2012), 419–454.

(bib-haah)=
## Haah: invertible subalgebras

Jeongwan Haah, [Invertible subalgebras](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.02086v4), Communications in Mathematical Physics **403** (2023), 661–698.

(bib-freed-hopkins)=
## Freed–Hopkins: invertible topological phases

Daniel S. Freed and Michael J. Hopkins, [Reflection positivity and invertible topological phases](https://arxiv.org/abs/1604.06527).
